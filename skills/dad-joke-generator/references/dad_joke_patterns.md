# Dad Joke Anatomy and Patterns

### 1. The Literal Switch
Take an idiom or common expression and resolve it literally.
- *Example*: "I asked my doctor if I could run with a cold. He said, 'Only if you can catch it.'"

### 2. The Homophone Twist
Substitutes words that sound identical but mean different things.
- *Example*: "What did the ocean say to the shore? Nothing, it just waved."

### 3. The Unnecessary Clarification
Stating the blazingly obvious in response to a rhetorical question.
- *Example*: "Why do birds fly south for winter? Because walking takes too long."

### 4. Golden Rules
- The pun should be immediately recognizable.
- Zero vulgarity, cynicism, or mean-spiritedness.
- Setup should be shorter than 2 sentences.
