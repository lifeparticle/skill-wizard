# Developmental Profile & Discipline Insights for Age 7

## Cognitive & Behavioral Characteristics
- **Sense of Fairness**: Highly attuned to rules and equity; will negotiate if rules feel arbitrary.
- **Self-Criticism & Perfectionism**: Can get easily discouraged or melt down if a task feels too hard or if they make a mistake.
- **Executive Functioning**: Working memory can hold roughly 3 instructions at once. Multi-step chores must be visually sequenced.
- **Internal vs External Motivation**: Responds best to visual checklists, gamification, and genuine effort praise ('process praise').

## Communication Best Practices
1. **Kneel to Eye Level**: Secure eye contact before giving instructions.
2. **Avoid Ambiguity**: Say 'Put your books on the bottom shelf' instead of 'Tidy up your mess'.
3. **Use When/Then Statements**: Replaces nagging with clear sequence logic.
